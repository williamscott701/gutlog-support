GutLog is a diary and an analysis tool, not a medical device. It records what you tell it and shows you statistical patterns in your own entries. It does not diagnose any condition, does not recommend or prescribe treatment, and is not a substitute for professional medical care.

The trigger findings, safe-food lists, lifestyle correlations and low-FODMAP guidance in the app are informational. Statistical association is not causation, findings can and do change as you log more, and what is true of people in general may not be true of you. Decisions about your diet, your medication and your treatment belong to you together with a doctor or dietitian who knows your situation. Restrictive diets, including the low-FODMAP diet, carry real nutritional risk and are best done with professional supervision; the elimination phase is not intended to be permanent.

Do not use GutLog in place of seeking care. Symptoms including blood in your stool, unexplained weight loss, fever, persistent vomiting, difficulty swallowing, anaemia, symptoms that wake you from sleep, or a first onset of symptoms later in life require medical attention rather than tracking. If you believe you are having a medical emergency, contact your local emergency services immediately.
